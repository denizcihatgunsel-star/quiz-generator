---

## Phase 1: Why examina.io Ranks and You Don't

Here's the honest truth: **examina.io has a massive domain age and authority advantage**, but their on-page SEO is actually quite weak. The reason they rank is likely:

1. **Domain name** — "examina.io" contains the exact keyword "examina" as the domain name
2. **Domain age** — They've been around longer (references to SmartExaminer legacy product)
3. **Backlinks** — Established business with clients (85,000+ exams, 64 schools)
4. **Brand name match** — Their brand IS "examina.io"

Your domain "examina.ink" also contains "examina" but uses a less common TLD (.ink vs .io). Google doesn't penalize TLDs, but .io has stronger association with tech products.

**The good news: their on-page SEO is terrible.** You can close this gap significantly.

## Phase 3: The Critical Gaps (What You Must Fix)

### HIGH IMPACT — Do These First

#### 1. Brand keyword "Examina" needs to appear more prominently
**Problem:** examina.io has the brand keyword in their domain, H1, and throughout copy ~20+ times. Your H1 doesn't contain "Examina" at all.

**Fix — Update your H1:**
```
Current:  "Turn any lesson into a quiz"
New:      "Examina — Turn Any Lesson into a Quiz with AI"
```

#### 2. Title tag should lead with "Examina"
**Problem:** For the keyword "examina", your title starts with "AI Quiz Generator". Google weighs words at the beginning of the title more heavily.

**Fix — Update title for brand keyword:**
```
Current:  "AI Quiz Generator — Turn Notes into Quizzes | Examina"
New:      "Examina — AI Quiz Generator | Turn Notes into Quizzes"
```

#### 3. Meta description should include "Examina" prominently
**Fix:**
```
Current:  "Generate quizzes from any text in seconds. AI quiz generator for multiple choice, flashcards, fill-in-the-blank & true/false questions. Free to try."
New:      "Examina is the AI quiz generator that turns any text into multiple choice, flashcards, fill-in-the-blank & true/false questions in seconds. Free to try."
```

#### 4. Add an "About Examina" section to the homepage
**Problem:** You have no copy that explicitly defines what "Examina" is as a brand/product. Google needs topical authority signals.

**Fix:** Add a section that starts with "Examina is..." — this helps Google associate the brand name with its definition.

#### 5. Create an /about page
**Problem:** examina.io has about content. You have no /about page. A dedicated about page with the brand name helps Google understand your entity.

#### 6. Add Organization schema with more detail
**Fix:** Add contact info, social media links, and founding date to your Organization schema.

### MEDIUM IMPACT

#### 7. Add image alt text everywhere
**Problem:** Neither site does this well, but you should fix it first.
**Fix:** Add descriptive alt text to your logo: `alt="Examina - AI Quiz Generator"`

#### 8. Internal linking with brand anchor text
**Problem:** Your landing pages link back to "/" but don't use "Examina" as anchor text.
**Fix:** Use "Examina" or "Examina AI Quiz Generator" as link text when linking to your homepage.

#### 9. Build a /about page with brand signals
**Fix:** Create a page at /about that describes Examina, its mission, team, and story. Include the word "Examina" naturally 8-10 times.

#### 10. Add a footer with brand reinforcement
**Problem:** examina.io has full contact info in footer. You have minimal footer.
**Fix:** Add a footer with "© 2026 Examina", contact email, and links to your landing pages.

### LOW IMPACT (but still do them)

#### 11. Add hreflang if targeting multiple regions
#### 12. Create a Google Business Profile for "Examina"
#### 13. Register "Examina" on social platforms (Twitter/X, LinkedIn) for brand signals
#### 14. Get listed on "alternatives to examina.io" pages (AlternativeTo, G2, etc.)

## The Real Strategy: You Can't Out-SEO a Domain Match

For the **exact keyword "examina"**, domain name match is a significant ranking factor. examina.io will always have an edge for this bare keyword because their domain IS the keyword.

**Your winning strategy is:**
1. **Rank for "examina quiz"** — you're more relevant
2. **Rank for "examina ai"** — they're not AI
3. **Rank for "examina app"** — you have a more modern app
4. **Rank for "examina ink"** — brand + domain variation
5. **Build topical authority** — your 6 landing pages + blog will generate long-tail traffic that examina.io can't match
6. **Get brand searches** — the more people search "examina quiz generator" or "examina ai", the more Google associates your site with those queries

They're a legacy assessment platform. You're a modern AI tool. **Don't compete on their keyword — make them compete on yours.**
